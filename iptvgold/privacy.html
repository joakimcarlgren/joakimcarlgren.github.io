IPTV Gold Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

IPTV Gold is a player for IPTV services the user already has. It provides no channels, movies or shows of its own, and it has no accounts: there is nothing to sign up for.

What stays on your device

Your provider's address, username and password, your favourites, watch progress, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. They are sent only to your own IPTV provider, never to me. On Apple TV, favourites and settings are also backed up to your own iCloud account. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device.

What the app sends me

To find bugs and see which features are used, the app uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics). It sends:

  • crash reports and error details;
  • which screens are opened and which buttons are used;
  • the titles of what is watched, and search terms typed in the app;
  • the message, if you choose to send feedback from the app's settings (links and email addresses are removed first);
  • device type, operating system version, app version and a random installation ID.

None of this includes your name, email address, provider address, username or password. The app does not use the advertising ID, shows no ads, and does not sell or share data with anyone. Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days and analytics data for at most 14 months. See Firebase's privacy information.

Children

IPTV Gold is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
